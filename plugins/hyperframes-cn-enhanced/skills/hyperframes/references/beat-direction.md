# Beat Direction

Plan the rhythm before writing HTML. A good HyperFrames video alternates quick hits, readable holds, and transitions with intent.

## Beat Plan

For multi-scene work, write a short rhythm line before implementation:

```text
fast-fast-HOLD-fast-SHADER-hold
```

Then map each scene to:

- Concept: what the viewer learns.
- Mood: urgent, calm, premium, playful, technical.
- Choreography verb: slam, drift, count, reveal, push, wipe, zoom.
- Transition: hard cut, push slide, blur crossfade, shader transition.
- Hold reason: why this frame deserves time on screen.

## Short-Video Rhythm

- Title scenes should land quickly and establish the hook in under 2 seconds.
- Data scenes need a readable hold after the number resolves.
- Do not stack multiple complex effects at the same timestamp.
- Use transitions to signal chapter changes, not between every minor detail.

## News Flash Rhythm

Use quick editorial beats: headline punch, photo reveal, data callout, takeaway. Keep narration and visuals aligned per scene, and let the measured audio duration decide the hold.
